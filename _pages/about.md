I am a Research Associate in the Department of Mechanical and Automation Engineering at The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK), supervised by Prof. Shing Shin Cheng (Apr. 2026 - Mar. 2027). I received my Ph.D. in Control Science and Engineering from Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST) in December 2025, advised by [Prof. Jian Huang](http://faculty.hust.edu.cn/huangjian2/zh_CN/index/1713465/list/index.htm), and my B.E. in Mechanical Engineering from Northeastern University in June 2018.

My research focuses on flexible medical robots for surgery and wearable robots for rehabilitation and assistance, including supernumerary robotic limbs (SRLs), exoskeletons, and other bio-inspired robots. My work spans mechanism design, optimization, and learning-based control.
